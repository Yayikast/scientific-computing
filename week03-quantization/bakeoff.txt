ref-bakeoff.csv — the week 3 reference bake-off, measured once for this course.

Four quants of the same 0.5B model (qwen2.5:0.5b-instruct-*), pulled from the registry.

Machine: Apple M4 Pro, 48 GB RAM, Ollama 0.31.2, temperature 0 via the API.
Speed: eval rate of a fixed 200-token generation; one cold run discarded, three warm runs kept.

Probes were frozen before any model ran, and every score is against the fp16 output as reference:

  p1_language   : write one Thai sentence explaining what PM2.5 is
                  (fp16 reference: a fluent Thai sentence, odd word choice)

  p2_arithmetic : What is 17 times 23? Reply with the number only.   (fp16 reference: 401)

  p3_recall     : What is the capital city of Australia? One word.   (fp16 reference: Sydney)

  p4_instruction: List the first five prime numbers, comma-separated. (fp16 reference: 2, 3, 5, 7, and 11)

Note: two reference outputs are themselves wrong (17x23 = 391; the capital is Canberra).
Scoring is against the fp16 OUTPUT, so the table measures degradation caused by quantization, 
not absolute truth. The 'notes' column quotes what the failing tiers actually did.
